The Android Automotive infotainment system uses a Qualcomm platform with
LP CAMM2 memory and DDR5 initialization handled by UEFI and EDK2. During boot,
the bootloader validates AVB, mounts UFS and eMMC storage, and loads the device
tree before starting the Linux kernel. Engineers use AIDL and HIDL interfaces,
SELinux policy, and Perfetto traces to investigate telemetry and performance.
On another board, Broadcom, Amlogic, and MediaTek components are compared;
BSP changes are built with Yocto, while ePSA and eDiags help diagnose hardware.
The issue is tracked in Buganizer, including tests for sPPR, hPPR, and LPCAMM2.
